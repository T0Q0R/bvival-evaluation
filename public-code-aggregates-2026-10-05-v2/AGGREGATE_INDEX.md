## Alias and stage cautions

- `score_uncertainty_only` historically names predicted current absolute-error risk, not predictive variance.
- `score_fixed_field_risk` is fixed-field risk routing, NOT fixed-field expected-benefit ranking.
- Turkey H2 is +0.496% versus its distinct post-error comparator; it is not the risk-routing comparison.
- The cohort flag `confirmatory_test_allowed_now=false` predates the authorized held-out readout.
- Frozen recipe `publicly_archived=false` flags describe creation-time status; see the root README.
- The 2% reference is author-selected, not an external business or publication threshold.
